Traction-breakout paper bot for the pump.fun replay sprint: it waits until a curve proves traction (at least 10 SOL of real reserves with at least 20 distinct non-creator buyers, price up at least 10% from create, creator not selling), buys the breakout once, adds once past 20 SOL of reserves, and exits on take-profit, stop, trailing stop, creator sell, staleness, or a time cap. Paper trading only, no real funds. Dev net PnL +1.0499 SOL on the kit's local grader; guardrails met; deterministic across two runs.
